# Finite-element reference for `disk` and `bearing`

Generated by `uv run tools/fem/run.py` (about four minutes, deterministic); the method, the
assumptions and the verification are in the docstrings of `tools/fem/*.py` and in
`tools/tests/test_fem.py` (needs `--with scikit-fem`, skipped without it).

- `fem_reference.csv`: the ratio f of each case at refinement levels 0–3 and a `converged` row
  (Richardson extrapolation when the three finest levels converge monotonically, else the finest
  value; `spread` is the range of the three finest levels). `disk`: f = ∫ p r dA / (N R), elastic
  layer of thickness H on a rigid base under a rigid flat punch, independent of E. `bearing`:
  f = ∫ p ds / |∫ p n ds|, plane-strain sleeve of wall 0.02 m around a rigid journal of 0.05 m,
  E = K t (1 + ν)(1 − 2ν)/(1 − ν) with K = 1e6 N/m³, loads in N per metre of length.
  `stiffness_vs_winkler`: the continuum's force (disk) or the Winkler sink over the continuum's
  (bearing), against the thin-layer Winkler mapping.
- `stop_times.csv`: the battery's base-variant stop times of `disk` and `bearing` (signed, from
  the viewer's export of the committed battery, checked against `results/battery.csv`) against
  the nominal theory and against t_FE = t_theory · f_nominal / f_FE (disk: the battery's slab,
  H/R = 4; bearing: the battery's load).
- `figures/`: the FE pressure distributions against the distributions of each scenario's band.
