conforming contact: volumetric and elastic-foundation laws on exact mesh intersections against

\abstract{Conforming contact, where the patch is not small against the bodies, is where
point-contact models are least reliable, and the model families that address it have not been
compared on common problems with known answers. We present a battery of
\result{battery/scenarios} scenarios with analytic references, classified by what each
reference rests on: a rigid-body result (class A), an assumed pressure distribution (B, read
against the band of admissible distributions) or the elastic-foundation model itself (C). On
it we run two elastic-foundation laws evaluated on exact mesh intersections, Gonthier's lumped
volumetric model and a distributed law that integrates the same foundation over the contact
surface, the quadratures of the literature, \law{pcm} and six native contact models of Drake, Chrono and MuJoCo.
On class B no elastic-foundation method is outside the band, while a face held at points of
its rim has the friction torque of a ring. The failures of the two laws are explained with
predictions that match the measurements: the rolling resistance of the damped foundation, a
friction-induced flutter, the stability bound of the explicit coupling. A closed-form Coulomb friction wrench for a linear
pressure on a triangle, the one new ingredient, makes friction independent of the
triangulation. Against a finite-element solution, the methods that integrate the foundation
correctly stop a disk spinning on a thick slab 18\,\% late, a limit of the foundation itself. There is no experimental reference, and the configurations of
the native models are ours and unreviewed. The work was carried out by an AI system (Claude
Fable~5.2, first author) directed and supervised by the second author.}

\keywords{conforming contact, volumetric contact model, elastic foundation, Coulomb friction,
benchmark, multibody dynamics}
